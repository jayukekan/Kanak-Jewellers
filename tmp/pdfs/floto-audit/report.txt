The website presents a clean, high-end aesthetic appropriate for a baby keepsake brand, with high-quality imagery and
a clear primary path. However, it suffers from several layout and consistency issues, including an unbalanced product
grid, inconsistent iconography styles, and non-standard placement of the chat widget. Refinement of the header spacing
and form alignments would significantly improve the overall professional polish.
